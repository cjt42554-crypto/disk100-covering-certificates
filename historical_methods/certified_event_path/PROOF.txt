1. Scope and concrete data

This package defines a new explicit PL path of 100 labeled circle centers.
The old independently certified rational configuration U=0.1155018241946 is
node -1. Nodes 0,...,21 are EXACT nearest-integer ties-to-even rounding at

The only historical numerical use is selection of these saved nodes and

On each consecutive segment, the path is exactly
  c_i(s)=((1-s) A_i+s B_i)/Q, 0<=s<=1.
All 100 centers remain strictly inside the unit disk, by convexity and the
exact node norm checks. Original files are read-only; the replay needs no

2. Exhaustive endpoint coverage on the WHOLE CLOSED UNIT DISK

For fixed distinct centers, f(x)=min_i |x-c_i|^2 is continuous on compact
closed D and has a maximum. An interior maximum cannot have one nearest
center: the corresponding strictly convex quadratic cannot have a local
maximum. If all nearest centers lie on a line (including exactly two), their
equidistance locus has a tangent direction in which the tied squared
distances are strictly convex, and inactive sites remain inactive nearby.
Thus an interior maximum has three noncollinear nearest centers and is one
of the enumerated rational triple circumcenters. The checker enumerates
EVERY noncollinear triple circumcenter inside the CLOSED disk, regardless of
Delaunay status; this conservative enumeration handles any topology/ties.

On the unit circle, all pair bisectors partition the circle into arcs with
a fixed nearest center. The squared distance along an arc is
  1+|c_i|^2-2 c_i dot x.
Its maximum occurs at an arc endpoint or the antipode of c_i. Constant arcs
for a zero center are covered by their endpoints or the extra point (1,0).
The checker enumerates both intersections of every pair bisector with the
unit circle (including tangent ties), all nonzero-center antipodes, and
(1,0). This list contains a whole-domain maximum; it is not a spatial grid.

Each candidate supplies a center witness, and exact arithmetic verifies its
squared distance <= U_node^2. Rational candidates use integer comparison;
boundary candidates use an exact sign of a+b sqrt(d), comparing a^2 and b^2 d
when signs differ. Thus all endpoints cover all of closed D. A floating-point
nearest-center calculation in build_source only proposes witnesses. Any
proposal fails closed unless exact comparison accepts; all 100 centers are
searched exactly if a proposal is rejected. The independent replay recomputes
the exhaustive candidate list and all witness comparisons.

3. Continuous coverage between endpoints

Let Dseg=(ceil sqrt(max_i |B_i-A_i|^2))/Q. The integer isqrt upward rounding is
checked independently; it is an upper bound on every labeled displacement.
For every x in closed D, a center covering x at A has distance <=U_A+s Dseg
along the segment. Independently a center covering x at B has distance
<=U_B+(1-s)Dseg. Hence the covering radius everywhere along the full segment
is bounded by the concave envelope
  g(s)=min(U_A+s Dseg, U_B+(1-s)Dseg).
Its EXACT maximum is min(U_A,U_B) for Dseg=0; U_B+Dseg if U_A>U_B+Dseg;
U_A+Dseg if U_B>U_A+Dseg; otherwise (U_A+U_B+Dseg)/2.
The verifier recomputes these formulas and the maximum across all segments.
No sampling can establish this claim; it follows from the triangle inequality
for every s and every x. Subdivision or optimizer stationarity is not assumed.
