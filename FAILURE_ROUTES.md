# Failed routes and limits of accepted results

A failed route means a saved finite investigation did not improve the certified record or settle the original problem. It does not exclude every configuration, an entire basin or an infinite search domain.

## Conditional-geometry corrections

The [conditional local lemmas](conditional_local_lemmas/README.md) preserve the hypotheses, counterexamples and corrections. Actual A cannot be replaced by independent J; the common tau must be paid completely and exactly once. Weak-arc and joint-variance counterexamples apply only in their reviewed domains. The broad three-face calculation `7/388` was incorrect; the corrected value is `35/1944`. Strict zero-product arguments, skew localization, cross-radius norm floors and a scalar factor of two were corrected. Coverage-preserving projection of a middle center does not preserve the chosen fan, circumcircle or structural labels.

The `.0228` value is an unreviewed candidate. The fresh guard was unreviewed, uncompiled and unexecuted, and its plan was cancelled. The proposed active-hole patch around `[47,55,88]` was not executed and supplies no certified event direction. Global admission and allocation remain unresolved.

All 27 completed original runs remain completed. The remaining 69 were not launched. No certified upper record improved during the final constructive phase, and the exact global optimum remains open.
